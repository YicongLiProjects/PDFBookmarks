Bookmarks help page

Bookmarks allow you to navigate quickly to specific pages in the currently 
opened PDF file. You can use them to open your PDF file where you left off.
The latest bookmark will be used for that purpose. 
1. Adding bookmarks
	To add a bookmark, just click the "add" button on the left 
	of the scroll viewer where the PDF is rendered. A new bookmark
	will be created at the current page.
2. Removing bookmarks
	To remove a bookmark, just select it in the list and click the
	"remove" button on the left of the scroll viewer where the
	PDF is rendered.
3. Editing bookmarks
	To edit a bookmark, just select it in the list and click the
	"edit" button on the left of the scroll viewer where the
	PDF is rendered. You can change its name and page number.
4. Adding / removing / editing notes
	You can create, edit and delete notes in the note editor which 
	can be accessed from the menu bar in the file viewer with the 
	"Notes" button.
